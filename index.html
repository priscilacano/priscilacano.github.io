About

Academic profile

My research integrates cropping systems, environmental characterization, and digital agriculture tools to improve resource-use efficiency and crop performance under variable environmental conditions.

I combine field experimentation, spatial data analysis, and producer decision-making research to develop sustainable, data-driven, and practical crop management strategies.

Research

Research interests

Cropping systems

Crop rotations, cover crops, sustainable intensification, and agronomic systems analysis.

Digital agriculture

Remote sensing, UAV and satellite imagery, farmer decision-making, and decision-support tools.

Spatial data analysis

Environmental characterization, envirotyping, climate and soil drivers of crop productivity.
